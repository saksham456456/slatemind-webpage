Cookies & Browser Storage

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This page explains what SlateMind stores on your device.

The website (this landing page)

It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.

The app

The app uses your browser’s local storage, not cookies, to keep things working. It is strictly functional:

  • The name you enter, your XP, level, streaks, badges and lesson history.
  • Your settings, such as lesson speed, sound and chalk theme.

This data stays on your device. We do not receive it and it is not used for advertising or tracking. Because it is essential to the features you use, no consent banner is shown. If we ever add analytics or other non-essential storage, we will ask for your consent first.

Clearing it

Use the “Reset” button in the app’s settings, or clear site data for this site in your browser settings. This permanently removes your progress.
