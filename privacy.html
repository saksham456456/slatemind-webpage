Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

SlateMind (“we”, “us”) is operated by {{OWNER_NAME}}. This policy explains what personal data the SlateMind website and app handle, why, and what your rights are under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). For the purposes of that Act, we act as the Data Fiduciary.

1. What we collect

Lesson topics and follow-up questionsThe text you type is sent to our server so a lesson can be generated.
Technical dataYour IP address is processed to apply rate limits and prevent abuse. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs.
Progress and settingsXP, streaks, badges, history and preferences are stored only in your own browser (see Cookies & storage). We do not receive them.

There are currently no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and we do not run advertising or analytics trackers. Please do not type personal information into lesson prompts.

2. Why we use it

  • To generate lessons, quizzes and answers you ask for.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to messages you send us.

3. Who processes it

  • Groq (AI inference provider): receives the text of your request to generate the lesson. Its servers may be outside India.
  • Vercel (hosting): delivers the site and runs our server code, and may keep technical logs.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. How long we keep it

We do not keep a database of lessons or user profiles. Data held by our providers (for example server logs) is kept according to their retention settings. Browser data stays on your device until you clear it.

5. Children and students

SlateMind is built for learners, including students under 18. Today it collects no account details. Before we add accounts, payments or any feature that stores a child’s personal data, we will add a verifiable parental or guardian consent step as the DPDP Act requires, and we will not track or profile children or show them targeted advertising.

6. Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you may nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf. You may also withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at https://saksham-mogha.vercel.app/contact and we will reply within a reasonable time, and in any case within the period the law requires. Because we hold no profile data, we may ask for the approximate time and content of a request to find it in logs.

7. Grievances

For any complaint about how your data is handled, contact {{OWNER_NAME}} at https://saksham-mogha.vercel.app/contact. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, say so on the home page.
